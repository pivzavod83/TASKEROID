Mission Briefing

Taskeroid is not a to-do list. It is a living space simulation — your work rendered as an asteroid field closing in on a planet. Deadlines become orbital pressure. Priority becomes gravitational mass.

The closer the deadline, the closer the asteroid gets. The higher the importance, the bigger the threat.

You do not just track tasks. You command a system under pressure.
